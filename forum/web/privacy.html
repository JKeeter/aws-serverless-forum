Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This site and its member forum are run by the site operator. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices. It is short because very little is collected.

Information collected

  • Account information — the email address and display name you provide when you register, or that Google/Facebook shares if you choose to sign in with them (your email and name only).
  • Content you post — the topics, replies, and any photos or files you upload to the forum.
  • Basic technical data — standard web server and CDN logs (e.g., IP address and browser type) used to operate and secure the site.

No payment information is collected, and there is no advertising or third-party tracking on this site.

How it is used

  • To create and secure your member account and let you log in.
  • To show your posts and attribute them to your display name.
  • To operate, maintain, and protect the forum from abuse.

Signing in with Google or Facebook

If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook," that provider verifies your identity and shares your name and email address so a member account can be created or matched. The site does not receive your password, your contacts, or any other data. You can always register with an email and password instead.

How your information is stored and shared

The site runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Account sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito, and data is stored in AWS services on the operator's behalf, strictly to operate the site.

Your personal information is not sold, rented, or traded. It is only shared if required by law or to protect the site and its members.

Cookies and local storage

When you log in, a sign-in token is stored in your browser's session storage so you stay logged in during your visit. It is cleared when you log out or close the tab. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your own posts while logged in.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated content — contact the site operator and an admin will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach the operator through the forum.
